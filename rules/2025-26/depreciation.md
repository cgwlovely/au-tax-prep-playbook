# 2025–26 · Depreciation · 折旧

> The dates and rates here are legislated rather than indexed, so they do not move year to year. They are restated in each year's directory for completeness — the `last_verified` date still records when someone actually read the source.

## DEP-01 — Div 43 capital works, residential · 资本工程

**Rule** — Eligibility turns on the construction **start** date:

| Construction started | Rate | Period |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Before 1985-07-18 | nil | — |
| 1985-07-18 to 1987-09-15 | 4% | 25 years (mostly exhausted) |
| On or after 1987-09-16 | 2.5% | 40 years |

Extensions, alterations and improvements are tested against **their own** start date. Structural improvements (driveways, retaining walls, fences, carports) qualify if started on or after 1992-02-26. Establish the construction year **before** commissioning a quantity surveyor.

**规则** — 资格取决于**开工日期**（见上表）。扩建、改建、改良按**各自的**开工日判定。结构性改良自 1992-02-26 起开工即合格。**先确认建成年份，再决定是否委托 quantity surveyor。**

**Source** — ITAA97 Div 43; ATO Capital works deductions
**Last verified** — 2026-09-23 · **Risk** — low

## DEP-02 — Who may estimate construction cost · 谁能估算建造成本

**Rule** — Use **actual** construction expenditure wherever it can be obtained. Only where it cannot is an estimate needed, and then only from an appropriately qualified person: a quantity surveyor, clerk of works, supervising architect, or a builder experienced in estimating that kind of work. Valuers, real estate agents, accountants, solicitors and the taxpayer are expressly excluded.

**规则** — 能取得**实际**建造成本的，用实际成本。**只有无法取得时**才需要估算，且只接受合格人士：quantity surveyor、clerk of works、supervising architect、有同类工程估算经验的 builder。估值师、中介、会计师、律师及纳税人本人均被明文排除。

**Source** — TR 97/25
**Last verified** — 2026-09-23 · **Risk** — low

## DEP-03 — Div 40, second-hand residential assets · 二手住宅设备

**Rule** — For second-hand residential property acquired on or after **2017-05-09**, assets already in the property at acquisition cannot be depreciated (s40-27). Assets the owner buys **new** and installs are unaffected.

**规则** — **2017-05-09** 之后取得的二手住宅，购入时已存在的资产不可计折旧（s40-27）。业主自购并安装的**全新**资产不受此限。

**Source** — ITAA97 s40-27
**Last verified** — 2026-09-23 · **Risk** — low

## DEP-04 — Low-cost thresholds and the low-value pool · 低成本阈值与低值资产池

**Rule** — Cost under **$300**: immediate deduction. Cost under **$1,000**: may be allocated to a low-value pool. First year in the pool is **18.75%** of cost and is **not** apportioned by days held; later years 37.5%. Once the pool is chosen, every subsequent low-cost asset must be allocated to it.

**规则** — 成本低于 **$300** 可即时全额扣除；低于 **$1,000** 可并入低值资产池。池内首年按成本的 **18.75%** 计，且**不按持有天数摊分**；此后各年 37.5%。选用低值池后，此后所有低成本资产必须一并并入。

**Source** — ITAA97 Div 40 Subdiv 40-E; ATO Low-value pool
**Last verified** — 2026-09-23 · **Risk** — low

## DEP-05 — Start time · 起算日

**Rule** — Depreciation starts when the asset is **first used or installed ready for use**, not when it was purchased (s40-60). An asset bought before year end but first used in the following year gives nil deduction in the year of purchase.

**规则** — 折旧自**首次使用或安装可供使用**之日起算，不是购买日（s40-60）。财年结束前购入、次年才启用的，购买当年折旧为零。

**Source** — ITAA97 s40-60
**Last verified** — 2026-09-23 · **Risk** — low
