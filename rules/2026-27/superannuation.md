## SUP-01 — Concessional contributions cap · 优惠供款上限

**Rule** — **$32,500** for 2026-27 (indexed in $2,500 increments). Unused cap from the previous five years can be carried forward where total super balance at 30 June of the previous year is under $500,000; unused amounts expire after five years, oldest first.

**规则** — 2026-27 为 **$32,500**（按 $2,500 递增指数化）。上一财年 6 月 30 日 TSB 低于 $500,000 的，可结转前五年未用额度；未用额度满五年失效，先进先出。

**Source** — ATO Concessional contributions cap; confirmed against a fund's member portal showing the current-year cap

## SUP-03 — General transfer balance cap · 转入上限

**Rule** — **$2.1 million** from 1 July 2026, up from $2.0 million; indexed in $100,000 increments. This is the ceiling on what one person can move into a tax-free retirement income stream. **It is per person and cannot be transferred, shared or pooled between spouses** — unused cap in one name is lost.

Someone starting a retirement income stream for the first time on or after 1 July 2026 gets a personal cap of $2.1 million. Existing pensioners who never fully used their cap get a proportional increase.

**规则** — 2026-07-01 起 **$210 万**（原 $200 万），按 $10 万递增指数化。这是一个人可转入零税退休金账户的上限。**按人计算，不可在配偶间转让、共享或合并** —— 一方未用掉的额度直接作废。

**Source** — https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/transfer-balance-cap
**Last verified** — 2026-10-26 · **Risk** — low

## SUP-04 — Division 296, tax on large balances · 大额余额附加税

**Rule** — **Now law, applying from 1 July 2026.** An additional **15%** applies to the proportion of earnings relating to a total super balance above the large super balance threshold of **$3 million**. A second threshold of **$10 million** attracts a further 10% on earnings above it. Both are indexed — the first in $150,000 increments, the second in $500,000. Assessments begin during 2027-28.

The threshold is **per person**, which is why a couple's balances being far apart costs more than the same total split evenly.

**规则** — **已成为法律，自 2026-07-01 起适用。** TSB 超过 **$300 万**的部分，其对应收益额外征收 **15%**；超过 **$1,000 万**的部分再加 10%。两个门槛均指数化（分别按 $15 万和 $50 万递增）。评税从 2027-28 年度开始发出。门槛**按人计算** —— 这正是夫妻余额悬殊比均分代价更高的原因。

**Source** — https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-and-super-professionals/for-superannuation-professionals/super-funds-newsroom/better-targeted-super-concessions-is-now-law
**Last verified** — 2026-10-26 · **Risk** — low

## SUP-05 — Downsizer contribution · 缩减住房供款

**Rule** — From age **55**, up to **$300,000 each** from the proceeds of selling a home owned for 10 years or more. Does **not** count toward the concessional or non-concessional caps. Must be made within 90 days of settlement. The combined total is capped at the actual sale proceeds. The contribution does become an assessable super asset for the Age Pension assets test.

**规则** — **55 岁**起，出售持有满 10 年的住房所得，**每人最多 $300,000** 可投入 super。**不占**优惠或非优惠供款上限。须在交割后 90 天内完成。两人合计不得超过实际售房所得。该供款会计入 Age Pension 资产测试。

**Source** — https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/super-for-individuals-and-families/super/growing-and-keeping-track-of-your-super/how-to-save-more-in-your-super/downsizer-super-contributions
