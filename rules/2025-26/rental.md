# 2025–26 · Rental property · 出租房

## RENT-01 — Report gross, itemise expenses · 毛额申报，逐项列支

**Rule** — Report the full gross rent as income and claim each deductible expense separately. The managing agent's net transfer is rent **less** what the agent already paid out; using it as income while also deducting the agent's Money Out double-counts.

**规则** — 收入按 gross rent 全额申报，费用逐项列支。中介的净转账是租金**减去**中介已代付的部分；用净额当收入、同时又扣除中介 Money Out，等于重复计算。

**Source** — ATO Rental properties guide; myTax Rent instructions
**Last verified** — 2026-09-23 · **Risk** — low

## RENT-02 — Insurance · 保险

**Rule** — Insurance on the rental property is immediately deductible, and covers building, contents, public liability and loss of rent. The test is what the policy insures, not whether the product is called "landlord insurance". Cover on your own home is not deductible here.

**规则** — 承保出租房的保险可当期扣除，涵盖 building、contents、public liability、loss of rent。判断标准是保单保什么，不是产品名称。自住房的保单不可扣。

**Source** — https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/investments-and-assets/property-and-land/residential-rental-properties/rental-expenses

## RENT-03 — Borrowing expenses · 借款费用

**Rule** — Deductible over **5 years or the term of the loan, whichever is shorter**. Where total eligible borrowing expenses are **$100 or less**, deduct in full in the year incurred. On full early repayment, the remaining balance is deductible in the year the loan is repaid.

**规则** — 按 **5 年或贷款期限，取较短者**摊销。合资格借款费用合计**不超过 $100** 的，当年全额扣除。提前全额还清的，余额在还清当年一次扣除。

**Source** — https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/investments-and-assets/property-and-land/residential-rental-properties/rental-expenses/borrowing-expenses
**Last verified** — 2026-09-23 · **Risk** — low

## RENT-04 — Reimbursements and recoupments · 补偿与赔付

**Rule** — A reimbursement or recoupment of deductible expenditure **may** be assessable — it is not automatic, and the character has to be established first. Where a tenant pays for damage and the repair is deducted, the **whole** amount received is income. Insurance proceeds on a capital item, rebates on a depreciating asset, and bond applied to arrears are each treated differently (cost base, balancing adjustment, or rent).

Classify every such receipt as `conditional` until determined. What is never acceptable is deducting the expense while leaving the recovery unreported.

**规则** — 可扣支出的补偿或赔付**可能**计入应税收入，并非一律如此，须先定性。租客就损坏付款、而维修支出已扣除的，收到的**全额**计入收入。资本性项目的保险理赔、折旧资产的补贴、押金抵欠租，三者处理各不相同。定性前一律标 `conditional`。支出照扣、补偿不报，任何情况下都不可接受。

**Source** — ATO Rental income (reimbursements and recoupments)
**Last verified** — 2026-09-23 · **Risk** — low

## RENT-05 — Travel to a residential rental property · 住宅出租房差旅

**Rule** — Not deductible for individuals from 2017-07-01, and not recognised in the cost base.

**规则** — 自 2017-07-01 起个人不可扣，且不计入 cost base。

## RENT-06 — Body corporate levies · 物业费

**Rule** — Ordinary administration fund and general-purpose sinking fund contributions are generally immediately deductible. A special levy raised for a specific capital improvement generally is not, and may become capital works once the project completes. Keep the notice as evidence of character.

**规则** — 普通 administration fund 与 general-purpose sinking fund 出资通常当期可扣。为特定资本改良筹集的 special levy 通常不可立即扣除，工程完成后可能按 capital works 处理。保留 notice 作为性质证明。
