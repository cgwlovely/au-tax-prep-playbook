# 2025–26 · Work expenses · 工作相关费用

## WFH-01 — Working from home, fixed rate · 居家办公固定费率

**Rule** — 70 cents per hour worked from home. Covers energy (electricity and gas), internet and data, mobile and home phone, stationery and computer consumables. None of those may also be claimed separately. Equipment depreciation remains claimable on top.

**规则** — 每在家工作一小时 70 分，涵盖电、燃气、宽带与数据、手机与固话、文具与耗材；这些项目不得再单独扣除。设备折旧可另行扣除。

**Source** — https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/income-deductions-offsets-and-records/deductions-you-can-claim/work-related-deductions/working-from-home-expenses/fixed-rate-method

## WFH-02 — Fixed rate record requirement · 固定费率法的记录要求

**Rule** — A record of the **total actual hours** worked from home for the **whole income year** (timesheet, roster, diary or similar). An estimate is not acceptable, and a representative four-week period is not sufficient. Applies from 2023-03-01 (PCG 2023/1).

**规则** — 必须保存**全年实际工时**记录（考勤表、值班表、日志等）。估算不被接受，四周代表期不够。自 2023-03-01 起适用（PCG 2023/1）。

**Source** — PCG 2023/1; ATO fixed rate method page (above)
**Last verified** — 2026-09-23 · **Risk** — low

## WFH-03 — Actual cost method records · 实际成本法的记录要求

**Rule** — Receipts and bills for each expense claimed, plus a record of hours worked from home — either actual hours, or a diary kept for a representative continuous four-week period showing the usual pattern. For an itemised phone or internet bill, the work-related percentage may be worked out over a continuous four weeks and applied across the year.

A dedicated home office is **not** a general precondition. Without one, each expense is still apportioned by work use. A dedicated room unlocks a narrow set of additional items, principally cleaning of that room (apportioned by floor area, then reduced for private and other-household use). Occupancy expenses are a separate and much harder test, generally unavailable to an employee.

**规则** — 所报每项费用的发票账单，加上工时记录（实际工时，或能体现常规模式的连续四周代表期日志）。逐项计费的话费网费，可按连续四周算出工作比例再套用全年。专用工作区域**不是**一般前提；没有专用房间同样按工作比例分摊。专用房间解锁的主要是该房间的清洁费。占用类费用是另一套判定，受雇人员通常不适用。

**Source** — https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/income-deductions-offsets-and-records/deductions-you-can-claim/work-related-deductions/working-from-home-expenses/actual-cost-method
**Last verified** — 2026-09-23 · **Risk** — low

## WFH-04 — Methods are mutually exclusive · 两法互斥

**Rule** — Fixed rate and actual cost cannot both be used for the same period. Choose per income year by calculating both against actual days at home and standard hours.

**规则** — 同一期间只能二选一。每个财年应按实际在家天数与标准工时分别试算后再选，不要沿用上一年。

**Source** — ATO working from home expenses (overview)
